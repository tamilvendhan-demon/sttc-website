# KALA LINK AI — 3-minute SIH Demo Script

Slide 1 — Problem & Elevator (20s)
- Title: Turn Your Craft Into Opportunity
- Statement: Many marginalized artisans lack digital skills to present and find buyers. Our platform Thedal gives them an AI business assistant.

Slide 2 — How Thedal Works (40s)
- Visual flow: Photo / Voice → Thedal analysis → Smart catalogue → Market matches → Buyer discovery
- Call out AI features: image understanding, catalogue generation, market linkage, price insight, voice input.

Live demo steps (90s)
1. Open landing page `/kala` — quick context (5s)
2. Click `Try Thedal` — show Thedal interface (5s)
3. Run `Run AI Demo` — show image analysis and catalogue JSON (25s)
4. Ask Thedal: "Who should I sell this product to?" — show market-match reply (10s)
5. Switch to `Add Product` — upload image, `Generate with AI`, edit title, `Publish Product` (30s)
6. Open `Marketplace` and show the published product (15s)

Slide 3 — Impact & Ask (25s)
- Impact: empowers artisans, low friction, multilingual and voice-first
- Ask: support for pilot deployments, access to buyer networks, mentoring and grants

Judge Q&A prompts (extra)
- Who are users? Artisans (primary), buyers (secondary), admins
- How do you ensure trust? Human verification + AI confidence indicators
- Why not Amazon? We are a business assistant, not a generic marketplace
